# Privacy foundations and retained-data boundaries

These are actual local data workflows, not a certification or a claim that jurisdiction-specific retention, provider deletion or backup erasure has been verified. Every route uses active tenant membership, no-store responses, and subject-specific selection. No privacy route exposes credentials or another subject's private report/comment body.

- `GET /v1/churches/:churchId/privacy/export` returns the requesting subject's live church membership, reactions, own discussions/reports/review comments, authored revision references/hashes, own review decisions, notifications, contact preferences and consent evidence. It excludes other subjects, encrypted phone values, secrets and church-owned teaching payloads. External IdP/provider records and backups are separately disclosed exclusions.
- `POST /privacy/requests` `{kind:'export'|'erasure'}` →202 `{id,kind,status:'requested',message}`. Requires CSRF and idempotency. It creates an actionable request, not a false deletion result.
- `GET /privacy/requests` returns the member's own requests; an explicit `privacy:manage` grant permits the church queue. Each row includes `completedAt`, `retentionReviewAt`, and `fulfillment` when processed.
- `POST /privacy/requests/:id/fulfill` requires `privacy:manage`, CSRF and idempotency. Body `{identityVerified:true,reason,retentionBasis?,retentionReviewAt?}`. Reason is at least ten characters. The operator must actually verify requester identity. Export fulfillment returns `{id,status:'completed',export,downloadPath}`. The exported data is selected for the requester, not the administrator.
- `GET /privacy/requests/:id/export` returns a fulfilled export to its subject or explicit privacy administrator only. Other members receive404.
- Erasure fulfillment additionally requires a documented retention basis (20+ characters) and a named retention review deadline within one year. A policy owner must transfer ownership first. Result is `completed_with_retention_review`, with `liveDataRemoved:true`, `fullErasureVerified:false`, `externalDeletionVerified:false`, `backupDeletionVerified:false`, the named reviewer, deadline and retained inventory.

Erasure atomically removes reactions, review-comment/mention/notification bodies, replaces live discussion bodies with privacy tombstones, scrubs report reasons, clears phone ciphertext and geography, suppresses the contact, appends a revocation event, increments consent version, cancels unsent intents, cancels pending provider jobs/releases reservations, and deactivates/pseudonymizes the church membership. A subject tombstone blocks routine reactivation. Other churches and the external OIDC account remain independent. Provider-accepted/submitting/unknown calls retain their evidence; the result does not claim an in-flight call was recalled.

The response explicitly inventories immutable teaching/approval/audit evidence with opaque subject references; immutable discussion/provider evidence; sender-scoped hashed suppression; in-flight delivery evidence; external records and backup copies. Original immutable discussion revisions and consent evidence are retained pending the named review. The provided retention basis is an operator record, not an automated legal determination. These records are restricted to applicable operational permissions; production retention/offboarding requires reviewed policy, processor procedures and access controls.

## Restore and retention procedure

Before restoring traffic, reconcile `privacy_tombstones`, privacy fulfillment records, sender-scoped hashes and revocation events from the latest operational ledger into the restored database. Keep erased memberships inactive and contacts suppressed; do not decrypt/send from a restored contact before that reconciliation. Verify with subject-specific queries and preserve a redacted audit of the reconciliation. The native backup/restore drill demonstrates database recovery, not automatic deletion-ledger replay or backup erasure.

Review `retentionReviewAt` obligations in the privacy queue. A privileged, independently authorized retention procedure may remove/redact retained immutable payloads after the church's actual policy and legal holds are resolved; the ordinary runtime cannot disable immutability or perform that purge. Automated retention purge, encrypted offsite backups, provider erasure receipts, external IdP deletion, and tested restore-ledger automation remain release gates. No endpoint marks those gates complete.
