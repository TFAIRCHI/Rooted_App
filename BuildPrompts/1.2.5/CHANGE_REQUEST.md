# Change Request 1.2.5

## User outcome

Contributors need a simple place to understand how published studies are being received by the church. They should select **Study Engagement** from the left navigation, browse previously delivered studies, and open one to see:

- the member-facing study reading experience;
- reaction totals by appreciation type;
- discussion activity and moderation status;
- delivery/publication date and channel evidence;
- privacy-safe trends rather than an unrestricted member directory.

## Constraints

- Church Members do not receive this workspace or its analytics.
- Contributors retain all current contributor permissions and may inspect only their own church tenant.
- Published content is immutable for the reading preview; edits create new editorial revisions and do not rewrite historical publications.
- Discussion moderation remains separate from study approval.
- Aggregates must not imply that a reaction or comment represents doctrinal endorsement.
- Empty, loading, unavailable-provider, disabled-discussion, and no-history states are first-class.

## Out of scope

Member-level surveillance dashboards, exporting personally identifiable engagement data, sentiment inference, automated pastoral conclusions, rankings of members, and new notification providers.
