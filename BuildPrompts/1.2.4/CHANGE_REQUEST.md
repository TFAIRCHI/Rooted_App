# Change Request 1.2.4

## Approved design scope for review

- Remove visible Gather branding from all member and contributor surfaces.
- Simplify product roles to Church Member and Contributor.
- Keep per-study approver assignment as a workflow relationship rather than a third product role.
- Reduce contributor primary navigation to Dashboard, Calendar, Create a Study, and Settings.
- Consolidate source, review, delivery, moderation, and people operations into the appropriate destination.
- Present the study lifecycle as Create Study → Gain Approval → Publish Study.
- Ensure published studies are available to members and notifications are asynchronous follow-up delivery.

## Execution boundary

This document defines the next build request. It is not an authorization to execute production changes until explicitly approved.
