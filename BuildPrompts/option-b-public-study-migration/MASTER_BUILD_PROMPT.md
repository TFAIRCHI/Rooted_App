## Nonnegotiable product behavior

- Canonical `/c/{slug}` and `/c/{slug}/studies/{publicationId}` work on a new device, in a private window, and from a forwarded SMS without reader sign-in. The church page lists current live, nonwithdrawn published studies. A withdrawn, deleted, future or superseded publication is not exposed by public reads or community APIs.
- No reader phone verification, magic-login token or member session is created by opening an SMS link. The URL is a public address. Staff workspace continues to require its current sign-in and MFA.
- Reaction works without name or account, with change/removal and transparent approximate counts. Comment requires nonblank name and body, labels the name as unverified, enters pending review, and appears publicly only after approval. Staff can moderate/reply/report-handle. Historic member comments never become public automatically.
- SMS contains the **registered church sender name**, creator-written introduction, title, server-generated canonical public URL and required opt-out wording. Show exact composed preview/segment estimate. Any change to approved body or destination invalidates pending approval. Send only to consent-audited, unsuppressed contacts in the appropriate sending window; STOP suppresses further sends.
- Preserve existing study authoring, human approval, scheduling, withdrawal, contributor authorization and tenant RLS. Do not expose private drafts, source notes, phones, consent evidence, raw visitor identifiers, or staff-only discussion records.
