# Change Request 1.1.4 — Church Identity and Tenant Branding

## Required outcomes

- A church administrator can edit the church name from Settings.
- The name is tenant-scoped, validated, audited, and immediately reflected in staff navigation, Settings, member headers, and member-facing copy where the church name is shown.
- Administrators can access the existing logo upload/theme workflow from Settings.
- The active church logo appears in strategic staff and member brand surfaces, including the staff navigation brand area and member header/brand area.
- Rooted remains the product/application name. Church name and logo are tenant identity, not a product rename.
- Existing permissions, logo validation, theme revisions, tenant isolation, and approval workflows remain intact.

## Non-goals

- Renaming Rooted per church.
- Uploading arbitrary HTML, CSS, JavaScript, or executable SVG.
- Changing study content, approval, publication, or notification behavior.
