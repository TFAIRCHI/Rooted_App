## Decisions proposed for approval

- **Delete study** applies only before first publication. Published studies become unique historical artifacts; **Withdraw from members** hides them while preserving history/engagement. A separate admin-only **Hard Delete** after withdrawal requires typed irreversible confirmation and a reason; see the amendment for retained audit and backup/provider limits.
- Organize by **month → numbered week with date range → dated study → Create Study / Gain Approval / Publish Study**. Keep sermon series as optional context. One final study approval remains the rule.
- Use Sunday–Saturday calendar weeks clipped at the month boundary. October 2026 begins with **Week 1 · Oct 1–3**, followed by **Week 2 · Oct 4–10**. The user's Oct 1–6 range is treated as an illustrative label; the exact week convention is an explicit assumption, not a verified preference.
- Automatically decline clearly hostile, insulting, harassing, or otherwise policy-breaking comments. Preserve respectful questions, disagreement, lament, and prayer requests. Uncertain comments stay private for human review. This interpretation of “negative” is a Recommendation for review; rejecting every expression of sadness or doubt would also reject legitimate discipleship conversations.
- Present two Dashboard sections: **Study Preparation** and **Member Engagement**. Count final study states once; report unique member opens as views, with a defined audience denominator. A view is not proof someone read or understood the study.
