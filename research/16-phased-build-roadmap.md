# Phased build roadmap

**Recommendation:** Retain the proposed editorial → AI → messaging → full integration progression, while moving integration feasibility and sender/translation discovery into Phase 0. Move a minimal safe publication path into Phase 1 so member reading is testable. Relative effort uses S/M/L/XL, comparing work packages rather than promising calendar dates; team capacity and vendor lead times remain Unknown.

## Prototype, MVP, and post-MVP

**Prototype:** Local HTML/CSS/JS demonstrates interaction and produces screenshots; it has no production authorization, durable server state, real AI or messages. **Core MVP:** Phase 1 establishes a usable manual editorial service. **Discipleship delivery MVP:** Phases 1–3 with AI optional; an SMS pilot can proceed without Phase 2 if contributors prefer manual content. **Post-MVP:** full sync/identity after feasibility proof and Phase 5 differentiation. The word “MVP” must name which of these is being promised to a pilot church.

## Gates and sequencing adjustments

Start sender registration and rights review early because they may have external lead times. A Church Center custom link can be configured during Phase 1 even when full Publishing sync remains Phase 4. Do not block manual use on an unproven identity integration. A single-church pilot still uses tenant keys and isolation tests from the start. Defer real-time simultaneous editing until observed conflict/recovery data justifies its cost.

Plan reviews and content approvals are never collapsed for schedule pressure. Release AI behind per-tenant flags; release discussion only when moderation ownership exists. Stage SMS with staff test numbers, then a small explicitly opted-in cohort, then the full consented audience. A deployment rollback must not reverse consent suppression or duplicate publication jobs.

## Deliverable ownership and open questions

Product lead owns scope and stakeholder validation; pastor owns doctrine; security/engineering owns invariant tests; church admin owns sender/consent onboarding; operations owns restore/alerts. The [backlog](backlog.md) supplies implementable stories and [acceptance scenarios](21-acceptance-criteria-and-test-scenarios.md) supply gate evidence.

Alternative: integrate everything before any editorial delivery; risks spending the pilot on vendor uncertainty. Alternative: launch AI first; cannot safely validate content authority without revision/review foundations. Dependencies: budget, staffing, cloud/legal/vendor accounts. Risks: calling prototype behavior production-ready, treating desk research as an API PoC, and conflating member engagement with church impact. **Unknown:** team availability, actual pilot commitments, billing/legal scope and language requirements; estimates should be re-sized after Phase 0, not converted to exact dates now.
