## Method and evidence

The church contributor, UX specialist, data engineer and church member agents inspect the source/baseline, research primary documentation, challenge proposals, run bounded local checks and inspect new rendered screens. These are simulated role perspectives and expert reviews, not recruited-user studies or measured efficiency gains.

Distinguish current-source evidence, historical local runtime evidence, new prototype/model evidence, provider documentation, architecture proposals and untested release gates. Historical parser/API tests cannot validate the rebuilt editor, real devices, live identity delivery or genuine generation. Source and result timestamps identify the evidence actually used.

## Invariants

- Import is simple; processing happens behind the interface. Only necessary source details or exceptions interrupt the contributor, not a compulsory per-file wizard.
- One semantic document contains teaching and visual composition. Specialized internal jobs do not imply separate products/drafts.
- Flexible layout preserves meaningful text, reading order, captions and usable phone reading; arbitrary coordinates alone are insufficient.
- Sources are evidence, not executable instructions. Tenant/private filtering precedes retrieval; deletion affects future requests without silently rewriting approved snapshots.
- Contributors apply AI proposals; authorized reviewers approve exact revisions. AI cannot publish or resolve an approver's request.
- Public decks require a separate approved rendition; member reading, enrollment and sharing remain distinct choices.
- Phone/name/directory matching cannot auto-merge identity or grant staff authority. QR carries routing context and enrollment policy.
- Autostart means muted inline playback under normal settings. Respect reduced motion and browser restrictions with an honest fallback.
