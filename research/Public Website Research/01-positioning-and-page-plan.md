# Positioning and homepage plan

## What someone arriving from Google should understand

Rooted helps a church carry its pastor’s teaching into the rest of the week through reviewed Bible studies and everyday application. The pastor’s message supplies the shared direction; the AI Study Co-writer helps develop simple requests or detailed notes into plans and editable drafts, making a repeatable study rhythm easier to prepare and share.

The first screen must answer three questions: What is this? Why would it help our church? What can I do next? The proposed headline is **“Let Sunday take root. All week long.”** The supporting sentence explicitly names Bible studies, rather than assuming a visitor understands an internal product term.

Primary audience: pastors, church leaders and the contributors who help prepare studies. Secondary audience: members and households who want to understand what they would receive. The public page represents Rooted as a platform; the church’s own name and branding belong in the product preview and member experience.

## Benefits mapped to the requested goals

| Requested benefit | Proposed message and visual |
|---|---|
| Connection between Sundays | A shared passage and common questions help the church continue a conversation throughout the week; the hero shows a sermon becoming several study moments |
| Prepare for an upcoming sermon | The **Before Sunday** tab shows members meeting a passage, discovering its context, reflecting and preparing a question before gathering |
| Go deeper after a sermon | The **After Sunday** tab revisits teaching through Scripture, reflection, household conversation and a practical response |
| Bring the pastor’s teaching into the home | A family illustration and a simple table question make household devotions tangible; copy also includes friends and other households |
| Explore what Sunday cannot fully cover | A benefit card and family section introduce supporting context and time to ask questions, without suggesting the sermon was inadequate |
| Easily develop studies with AI | A dedicated co-writer section shows short-prompt and detailed-notes starting points, conversational planning, complete saved drafts, and brief human-control safeguards |
| Make this achievable for the church team | A four-part process connects notes, assisted drafting, human review and the member experience; no one-click autonomous publication claim |

These are proposed benefits and product intentions, not independently measured Rooted outcomes. There are no fabricated adoption counts, success percentages, endorsements or customer quotes.

## Homepage narrative

1. **Hero:** Rooted’s name, clear sermon-to-study description, original sermon/phone illustration, “Get started with your church” and “See how it works.” A returning visitor can sign in immediately.
2. **Three benefit cards:** stay connected; prepare hearts for the next sermon; explore the message more deeply. Each uses an original line icon and distinct accent color.
3. **AI Study Co-writer:** a prominent section explains how a short idea or detailed notes become a discussed plan and complete drafts. Switchable examples show both starting points. A small safeguards strip explains confirmation, human review and private church context. [AI content specification](05-ai-assisted-study-creation.md).
4. **An example week:** interactive Before/After Sunday tabs. The visitor can see the difference between preparing for a message and applying one. The example shows four weekday Scripture moments; the church chooses its actual schedule.
5. **The kitchen table:** a warm original illustration, concrete family use, and a link to a sample devotional. The question is practical rather than another feature description.
6. **How it works:** bring the message → shape the week → review and share → grow through the week. The diagram explains how Rooted reduces the organizational gap between an existing sermon and ongoing study.
7. **Power of 4:** a four-day visual and a restrained, attributed research summary. [Evidence and copy rules](02-power-of-four-evidence.md) govern this section.
8. **FAQ:** upcoming sermons, study frequency, church control, unresolved pricing and the member join path. Native expandable answers keep the page approachable.
9. **Closing invitation:** the visitor has enough context to begin. Repeat the same primary action and avoid a competing newsletter or AI-generation offer.

## What the process actually promises

The app has assisted drafting and a church review/publication workflow. Describe the co-writer as help for preparing and refining drafts. Do not say it independently determines doctrine, verifies every reference, publishes a week automatically, or guarantees discipleship results.

Start with summary notes or an outline. Full-length transcript processing, automatic YouTube ingestion and live transcription are not marketed as completed capabilities. The existing long-input limits remain a separate implementation topic.

Upcoming-sermon preparation is a scheduling and editorial use case: the pastor provides the passage or outline early. Rooted does not predict a sermon or obtain private notes automatically. Member access is through the church’s published experience; automatic SMS delivery is not presented as a default, connected service.

## The public search result

Proposed title: **Rooted — Bible studies that carry Sunday into every day**. Proposed description: **Help your church stay connected all week with pastor-led Bible studies, practical reflection, and devotions for the home. Explore Rooted and get started.**

The production public homepage should be accessible without signing in, have a single descriptive H1, render useful content for crawlers, and link to the canonical public domain once that domain is chosen. Keep church/member and workspace pages outside public indexing. Do not add a pricing `Offer`, fabricated reviews or customer counts to structured data. The included prototype is intentionally `noindex`.

## Tone

Hopeful, warm and specific. Make growth feel possible, not like another performance target. Use “your church,” “your people,” “a little time” and “a shared direction.” Avoid implying missed days invalidate someone’s faith or that a church must create seven new studies every week.
