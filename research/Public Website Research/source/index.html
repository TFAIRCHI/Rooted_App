THE POWER OF 4 · CENTER FOR BIBLE ENGAGEMENT

Four days.
A deeper rhythm.

CBE’s research links engaging with Scripture on four or more days a week with stronger measures of spiritual growth.

Rooted helps your church make room for that rhythm: coming back to the Bible, reflecting on its meaning, and putting it into practice.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Made for your
church’s real week.

You bring the teaching and the people. Rooted helps you connect the two.

Can we prepare for a sermon that hasn’t happened yet?

Yes. Start with the passage and teaching outline your pastor wants to introduce. Choose study dates leading up to Sunday, then review the content before sharing it.

How does AI help us create studies?

Begin with a simple request or bring detailed sermon notes. The AI co-writer helps clarify your goals, organize a multi-day plan, and prepare complete editable drafts. Your team confirms the direction and each day, checks the teaching and Scripture, and decides when to publish.

Explore the Study Co-writer →
Do we have to create a study every day?

No. Choose a rhythm your team and congregation can sustain. You can encourage Scripture engagement throughout the week without making every day a new production.

Who decides what our members see?

Your church does. Drafting, review, and publication are separate steps. Your church’s workspace and member experience remain distinct from other churches.

What does Rooted cost?

We’re finalizing plan options. You’ll see clear pricing before choosing any paid plan.

What if I’m a member, not a church leader?

Use the church link or invitation your church shares with you. You can join your church’s experience without setting up a new church workspace.
