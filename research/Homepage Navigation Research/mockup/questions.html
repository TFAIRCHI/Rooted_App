A FEW PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Made for your church’s real week.

Choose a question for more detail. The essentials stay in view.

Can we prepare before a sermon happens?+

Yes. Start with an upcoming teaching outline or passage, then prepare studies that help your people anticipate the message.

How does AI help?+

The co-writer helps plan and draft from your context. Confirm the plan and each day before saving editable drafts. Your team reviews Scripture, teaching, and wording before publication. Conversations stay scoped to your account and church. See an example →

Do we need a study every day?+

No. Choose a rhythm that fits your church. Four days is an invitation to regular Scripture engagement, not a requirement.

Who decides what members see?+

Your church’s authorized team manages review and publication. An AI draft is not automatically a published study.

What does Rooted cost?+

We’re finalizing plan options. You’ll see clear pricing before choosing any paid plan.
