BIBLE ENGAGEMENT RESEARCH

Why encourage four days?

The Center for Bible Engagement (CBE) reports that Scripture engagement four or more days a week is associated with higher odds of:

Giving financially to a church
+416%
Sharing faith
+228%
Discipling others
+231%

Survey associations, not measured Rooted results or guaranteed outcomes. These describe reported odds, not percentage increases in giving amounts.

Engagement includes considering and responding to Scripture. Four app visits alone do not measure it. CBE is cited as a research source, not a Rooted partner.
