SAMPLE STUDY · JOHN 15:1–8

Remain in Him.

A short example of the reading experience. No sign-in needed.

Begin with the passage.

Read John 15:1–8 in your Bible. Notice the relationship between the vine and its branches. Jesus invites His disciples to remain in Him.

Bring it into today.

We often measure growth by how much we can accomplish. This passage invites a different starting point: staying close to Christ.

PAUSE & REFLECT

Where are you relying on your own strength?

Name one part of your day to bring to Christ in prayer. What would it look like to stay connected to Him there?

One next step
Take a quiet moment before your next task to pray and return your attention to Him.
