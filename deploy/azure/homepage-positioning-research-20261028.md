# Rooted homepage positioning research

Prepared October 28, 2026 for the requested homepage revision. This is an internal research and copy-direction record. Competitor names and competitor feature descriptions belong in this document only, not on the public homepage. Official vendor material establishes advertised capability, not independently measured effectiveness.

## CBE evidence and appropriate public claims

Primary requested source: [CBE, Bible Engagement and The Power of 4](https://www.odbm.org/en/center-for-bible-engagement/blog/bible-engagement-and-the-power-of-4-a-key-to-spiritual-growth).

The synthesis covers mixed internet surveys totaling 108,196 people across eight years; the respondent count is not a common denominator established for each statistic. The reported relationships concern Scripture engagement on four or more days weekly. For proactive faith, models control age, gender, church attendance and prayer practices.

| Finding | Faithful presentation | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Sharing faith +228% | 228% higher reported odds of sharing faith | 228% more people sharing faith; conversion-rate uplift |
| Discipling others +231% | 231% higher reported odds of discipling others | 231% more disciples created |
| Loneliness −30% | 30% lower reported odds of loneliness | 30% less loneliness for Rooted users |

The source frames both positive and struggle findings as **odds**, not absolute probability or percentage-point changes. Its limitations explicitly reject a causal conclusion and automatic guaranteed transformation. These are not results from Rooted users, a Rooted efficacy study, or a financial ROI calculation. [Primary source](https://www.odbm.org/en/center-for-bible-engagement/blog/bible-engagement-and-the-power-of-4-a-key-to-spiritual-growth).

Short source excerpts for editorial verification: “significantly higher odds” and “does not allow us to conclude that Bible engagement causes”. Total direct excerpt length is intentionally brief.

### Recommended hero treatment

Place a compact three-card research strip directly after the hero's main promise/illustration and before the next full section. Keep the hero's sermon-to-everyday message and primary action visually dominant. A heading such as **A small rhythm. A meaningful reason.** gives the numbers a pastoral context without turning people into conversion metrics.

Recommended shared heading: **In CBE research, Scripture engagement 4+ days a week was associated with…**

Recommended cards:

- **+228%** — higher reported odds of sharing faith.
- **+231%** — higher reported odds of discipling others.
- **−30%** — lower reported odds of loneliness.

Recommended visible source line: **CBE survey findings describe associations, not guaranteed results from Rooted. Read the research ↗** Link directly to the supplied CBE article. The existing longer Power of 4 section should retain the distinction between meaningful Scripture engagement and opening an app. Do not claim CBE endorses Rooted.

Sales interpretation: lead with the human meaning—faith shared, relationships deepened and people less isolated. Rooted supplies a practical weekly workflow for a church to pursue that rhythm. Avoid donation-uplift statistics in the hero; their commercial prominence would shift the page away from serving people. No study reviewed measures monetary ROI for Rooted.

## Five competitors and adjacent alternatives

### 1. YouVersion for Churches

Official evidence: [YouVersion for Churches](https://www.youversion.church/) and [integrating Bible Plans](https://www.youversion.church/post/3-ways-to-integrate-bible-plans-into-your-church).

Advertised offering includes church profiles, featured Bible Plans, Scripture features, prayer and community plan participation. Its official guidance explicitly recommends sermon-relevant plans and Plans with Friends. Do not imply it lacks church community, Bible habits, or ways to support Sunday teaching.

Rooted positioning opportunity: explain the focused process of turning the church's own supplied teaching into an edited weekly study, with clear contributor and approver responsibilities. Present this as Rooted's chosen workflow rather than a claim that YouVersion cannot support any similar use.

### 2. Dwell for Churches

Official evidence: [Dwell for Churches](https://dwellapp.io/churches).

Advertised offering includes congregational audio Bible access, custom Bible plans/playlists and church administrators. It already connects a congregation around Scripture throughout the week. Audio accessibility is an authentic strength, so do not suggest Rooted uniquely offers a shared rhythm or is a superior audio Bible.

Rooted positioning opportunity: spotlight the church's source teaching, reflection/application and review-to-publication responsibilities. Do not compete on unsupported claims about audio libraries, translations or accessibility superiority.

### 3. RightNow Media

Official evidence: [RightNow Media small-group resources](https://www.rightnowmedia.org/small-groups) and [official library](https://app.rightnowmedia.org/).

Advertised offering centers on video Bible-study series, group resources, roadmaps and virtual Watch Together. Its content breadth and established teacher library are strengths. Do not imply its material cannot be used by local churches or alongside sermons.

Rooted positioning opportunity: emphasize continuity with the local church's current teaching and a short weekday reading experience. This is a focus distinction, not a claim that an extensive teaching library has no pastoral value.

### 4. Subsplash

Official evidence: [Custom church apps](https://www.subsplash.com/product/custom-church-apps), [platform overview](https://www.subsplash.com/) and [2026 church app features](https://www.subsplash.com/blog/best-church-app-features).

Advertised offering includes branded apps, media, Bible/reading plans, notes, messaging, notifications, giving and AI tools. Its 2026 material explicitly includes sermon-based AI discipleship content. Do not claim Rooted is the only church-branded experience or only platform combining AI and discipleship.

Rooted positioning opportunity: explain a deliberately concentrated sermon-to-study workflow and visible human review. Avoid declaring that broader platforms lack accountability without direct evidence.

### 5. Sermon Shots

Official evidence: [Sermon Shots](https://sermonshots.com/), [API capabilities](https://sermonshots.com/api/) and [official updates](https://sermonshots.com/updates/).

Advertised offering already includes sermon-derived five-day devotionals, discussion guides, summaries, social assets and shareable sermon pages. Updates describe saved guide templates and denominational/Bible-version preferences. It is the closest content-generation competitor in this set; sermon-to-devotional AI alone is not a defensible uniqueness claim.

Rooted positioning opportunity: show the whole member destination and editorial process around a generated draft: source context, plan, content review, approved calendar and member participation. Do not imply other tools generate unreviewable content or claim categorical superiority.

## Public homepage strengths supported by this application

Recommended section heading: **Built for the week after your sermon.** Optional lead: **One shared direction, from your team's preparation to your people's everyday lives.**

Use four concrete strengths with positive wording rather than an unnamed competitor attack:

1. **Your church's teaching stays at the center.** Start with the sermon/series and build weekday Scripture moments around its direction.
2. **AI helps draft. Your team leads.** Keep editing and human approval visible. Generated content is a draft, not pastoral authority.
3. **A clear path from preparation to publication.** Show plan/content review, responsibilities, dates and publication state in the real workspace.
4. **A simple place for people to respond.** Pair the church's approved reading experience with reflection/application and enabled participation; keep member navigation distinct from editing responsibilities.

This is a stronger-fit argument for churches wanting this particular workflow, not proof Rooted is universally better. Do not publish price superiority, user counts, testimonials, claimed minutes saved, uptime, spiritual-growth performance, automated delivery/provider claims or market exclusivity without evidence.

Local capability references reviewed: `README.md` documents sermon/series roles, independent approval, member published snapshots; `frontend/components/content-editor.tsx` exposes final review and approved/scheduled/published states; `frontend/components/dashboard.tsx` shows weekly readiness; `backend/src/api/cowriter-routes.ts` gates explicit drafts and budgets. Live Azure AI, scheduled publication and wider pilot acceptance remain unfinished per the testing resume, so public wording should not claim those were production-certified.

## Demo/showcase guidance

Use real local application pages with clearly identified fictional demonstration data. A segmented control such as **For your members / For your team** should switch the visible screenshots or live safe sample views; secondary controls may show reading, reflection, preparation and approval. Include a meaningful caption naming what the viewer is seeing and one short benefit statement.

Capture the actual member reader and actual contributor/dashboard/editor UI. Do not generate imaginary application screenshots or place a fabricated mockup under a real-demo label. If demo data has been shaped for readability, disclose it as sample content. Keep real identities, invitation tokens, production secrets and member information out of screenshots. A preselected initial screenshot avoids a blank-loading section. Keyboard focus and accessible names matter more than autoplay motion; respect reduced-motion preferences.

Use evidence-led copy close to each visual: **See the study your people will read** and **See how your team prepares it**. The demo's purpose is to reduce uncertainty about the product and make the member/editor distinction tangible. A click-through tour should lead to safe demo content or the existing fictional sample, not silently create production data.

## Giving and attendance copy revision

CBE’s Proactive Faith section reports giving financially to a church +416%, explicitly as higher odds after controlling for age, gender, attendance and prayer. This does not mean giving amounts rise 416%. The homepage now uses that stat instead of loneliness. The same synthesis does not quantify increased attendance; public attendance language is an invitation/goal, not a reported effect.
